Our Story

Born on the water

Lake Conroe Escapes began with a simple belief: the lake is the best place to bring people together — for business, for family, and for celebration.

Lake Conroe, Texas

Where every gathering becomes a memory

We started because we kept noticing the same thing: the most memorable business outings, the most joyful reunions, the most talked-about celebrations — they always happened near the water. Lake Conroe, just 45 miles north of Houston, is one of Texas's most beautiful lakes. We decided to make it the setting for extraordinary group experiences.

Today we offer fully curated packages that combine private boating on Lake Conroe with championship golf, exclusive private club access, and fine lakeside dining. We handle every detail — the boats, the crew, the tee times, the table reservations — so your group arrives and simply enjoys.

From 10-person corporate teams to 80-person family reunions, we've done it all. And we treat every event like it's the only one happening that day — because for your group, it is.

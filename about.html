Our Story

Two decades on the water

What began as a single yacht in Monaco has grown — quietly — into a small, devoted family of vessels and the people who run them.

Founded 2005

From one yacht to a quiet legacy

Maris was founded in 2005 by Captain Tomás Reverte, a third-generation mariner who had spent two decades commanding superyachts in the Mediterranean. He believed that a charter should feel less like a service and more like a stay with friends who happen to know every cove on the coast.

We've grown carefully since. Today our fleet stretches from Monaco to the Caribbean, but the rules haven't changed: we own or directly manage every vessel we offer, our crews stay with us for years, and no charter leaves the dock without a personal note from someone who knows your name.

It's still a small business. That's the point.
